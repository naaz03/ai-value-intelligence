AI Value Intelligence

Measure adoption. Prove value. Optimize AI investment.
“The AI gold rush is over. The audit has begun.” This product is that audit, built and working.
The intent

Give client leadership a clear and defensible answer to one question: is our AI investment paying back, and where should the next dollar go? And give us a working product, not a slide, that turns our AI value point of view into client conversations and paid engagements.

The problem

Every enterprise bought the licenses. Few can prove the return. Dashboards count prompts, active users and hours saved, yet none of these is ROI.

The solution

A platform that follows every AI dollar through to realized value, then tells leadership what to scale, fix or stop.

The approach

Keep potential, demonstrated and realized value separate. Count only realized value, measured against the full cost of AI, use case by use case.

What it does today

  • Executive overview: spend against realized value, ROI, payback and value leakage.
  • Full cost of AI: licenses, infrastructure, implementation, training, support, security and governance.
  • License optimization: finds unused, underused and overlapping seats.
  • Portfolio view: ROI by business unit and by use case.
  • Forecasts and a scenario simulator to test investment choices.
  • Open methodology: every formula explained and searchable, on desktop or mobile.
