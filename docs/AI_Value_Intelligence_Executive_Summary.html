The problem

Al Noor Holdings has invested $19.57M in AI, but usage dashboards cannot tell leadership whether that spend is creating value. Prompts are not productivity, hours saved are not cash, and adoption is not ROI.

The solution

AI Value Intelligence follows every dollar from investment to finance-realized value. Today $29.25M of $93.66M potential value is realized: 49.5% ROI with ~23-month payback, and $790K a year is recoverable from idle licenses.

Our approach

We measure every stage of the AI value chain and report value in three tiers: Potential, Demonstrated and Realized. Only Realized counts toward ROI, measured against the full cost of AI. Each of the 84 use cases is ranked Scale, Optimize, Monitor, Reassess or Stop.

What the Managing Director sees first

Portfolio value, net benefit and strategic investment decisions.

The value chain we measure

Activity is a leading indicator. Only the last steps are value.

AI Investment→Availability→Adoption→ Effective Usage→Operational Improvement→ Demonstrated Benefit→Financial Realization→ Realized Value→ROI
